How it works

The bulb is your meter: the longer it stays lit, the more energy the fuel produced.

1

Burn the waste

Weigh your fuel in grams and burn it in the prototype chamber.

2

Heat becomes power

Energy = mass × heating value, and a small share becomes electricity.

3

Time the bulb

Seconds lit × bulb watts gives the real energy. Compare it with the prediction.

How much energy can it generate?

Pick a waste, enter the grams, and see the prediction.

–Heat energy in the waste (MJ)
–Estimated electricity (J)
–Bulb time (s)
–Heat energy in kWh

Energy = grams × heating value. Electricity = heat × efficiency. Bulb time = electricity ÷ bulb watts. Efficiency is your own assumption; tune it using your real runs.

Actual energy = bulb watts × seconds lit. Demo runs are sample numbers, not real results.

Heating values (LHV) are literature figures from the Ahmedabad, Ilorin and Mbeya City studies, not our own measurements. Dry leaves use the plant trimmings value. Sawdust is not in the dataset and is estimated as equal to wood. Moisture lowers real yield.
